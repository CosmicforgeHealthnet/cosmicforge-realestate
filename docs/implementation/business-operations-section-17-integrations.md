# Business Operations Section 17 — Internal Service Integration Matrix

All calls use the shared signed service identity (`serviceauth`, Ed25519 service JWT with audience) over `shared/go/http` clients with timeouts; inbound internal routes require a signed caller allowlist plus organisation delegation. No integration reads another service's database. No endpoint is invented: where an owning service has no published contract, the Business side is a typed port that fails closed.
