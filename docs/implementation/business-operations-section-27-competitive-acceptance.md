# Business Operations — Section 27 competitive acceptance (sourced)

Competitor sources were read on **2026-11-13**. All are public pages listed in the source register at the end.

## How to read the verdicts

There are two separate verdicts per criterion:

* **Repository coverage** says what this repository implements and tests.
  * `PASS_VERIFIED` means real handlers, PostgreSQL integration tests, and process E2E tests where noted.
  * `PASS_INTERNAL / EXTERNAL_ACTIVATION_PENDING` means the internal behaviour is verified, but a third party must still issue access or credentials.
* **Competitive claim** compares against Booking.com and Airbnb host tooling.
  * `OBJECTIVE_COVERAGE` means a documented capability exists in CosmicForge that the cited competitor documentation does not describe, or describes more narrowly. This compares documented features, not quality.
  * `PARITY_OR_BETTER_UNPROVEN` means the comparison depends on outcomes such as forecast accuracy, revenue uplift, guest satisfaction or response time. These were **not measured**, because there is no production data, no A/B test and no access to competitor accounts.
  * `NOT_COMPARABLE` means the competitor does not publish a comparable capability.

No statement here says CosmicForge is *better* than Booking.com or Airbnb in market outcomes. Superiority in revenue, conversion or satisfaction remains **unproven** until live operation produces data.

## CF Meet (criterion 6) — inspection result

* **Repository inspected:** `CosmicforgeHealthnet/cfmeet` at commit `b9dc15be072107b79bac8c49ca6b4b9ed65d12ca`. Its public Messaging contract (`packages/contracts/bundled/public/messaging/v1.openapi.json`, version 1.16.0, server `https://api.cfmeet.com`) defines the operations Business needs:
  * `POST /v1/conversations` with `Idempotency-Key` and `CF-Acting-Principal`, and an opaque `ContextRef {namespace, reference}`;
  * `GET /v1/conversations/{id}`;
  * `POST` and `DELETE /v1/conversations/{id}/members`;
  * authentication by a `customerCredential` bearer verified by Platform Access.
* **Connected:**
  * Business now speaks this contract (`cfmeet.Client`). It is wired in the API and worker when `CFMEET_API_BASE_URL`, `CFMEET_API_CREDENTIAL` and `CFMEET_ACTING_PRINCIPAL_ID` are set.
  * Only the opaque reference `communication-context:<id>` leaves Business. The subject, property, reservation and message bodies stay in Business.
  * Without configuration, the client stays `Unavailable` and contexts remain `PENDING_AUTHORITY`.
* **External activation required** (owned by CF Meet, not this repository):
  1. A Platform Access project, environment and service-account credential with `conversations.create` and `conversations.manage` scopes for Real Estate.
  2. A BOT_OR_SERVICE acting principal.
  3. Principal issuance for Real Estate accounts. CF Meet spec §102/§104: `POST /internal/v1/principals/ensure` is restricted to CF Meet's FIRST_PARTY login authority under CF Meet service auth.

  Until principals exist, participants are returned as `PendingParticipants` and are not admitted.
* **Process E2E:** not run. Running CF Meet means building its Rust services (messaging, platform-access, gateway) and provisioning Platform Access, which is outside this repository. Per the mission rule, the contract double does not count as process E2E.
