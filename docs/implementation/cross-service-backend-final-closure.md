* JetStream consumers of Business events;

## 2. Marketplace customer side

Marketplace covers the customer journey:

* identity (own account authority);
* discovery of published supply;
* availability and canonical price readback from Business;
* holds and reservations requested from Business;
* payment intent and checkout through the Payment Service;
* status and cancellation;
* completed-stay eligibility and verified reviews;
* funnel events;
* secure reconciliation.

It owns **no** inventory, reservation or payment authority:

* Business issues the quote (`/internal/v1/business-supply/channel-quotes`) and owns the reservation.
* Payment settles the intent through the signed provider webhook.
* Business auto-confirms only on a VERIFIED collection.

## 5. Real-process gaps found and fixed in this mission

1. Business `CapacityAuthority` was never wired, so every commercial gate returned 503. Fixed with the Verification `OPERATING_CAPACITY` type, wired in the API and worker.
2. Verification dispatches only after `/process`. Business KYB and professional verification never called it. Fixed.
3. Property-authority `create_case` never attached evidence. Fixed (`evidence_media_id`, `evidence_type`).
4. Marketplace lacked the WEBHOOK capability that Business receipts require. Fixed.
5. Marketplace-initiated collection required a staff session. Fixed with a channel collection plus auto-confirm on VERIFIED.
6. `domainkit.CallBusiness` forwarded the end-user actor and escaped the query string into the path. Fixed.
7. Marketplace tried to read Payment intents it may not read. Redesigned: the customer reads through the gateway and settlement arrives by signed webhook.
8. Partner commit race (version conflicts on concurrent consumers). Fixed with state-checked moves.
9. Individual reservation guest registration was missing. Added `/reservations/{id}/guest-registrations`.
10. **Closure CLOSE was unreachable.** ENTER_RETENTION moves the organisation to `CLOSURE_PENDING`, where every write capability is restricted, but CLOSE required `organisation:write`. CLOSE now requires `organisation:verify` (MFA and a distinct actor) and is tested end to end.
11. **Annex D.36 partner runtime was missing.** Credentials existed only as configuration. Added the partner API (migration 00039): partner-key fingerprint authentication, client scopes, a cross-replica per-credential rate window, SANDBOX fixtures, PRODUCTION organisation isolation, and dual keys during rotation (`TestAnnexDPartnerAPIScopesRateLimitSandboxRotationAndRevocation`).
12. Untested Annex D transitions now have tests: utility accounts, notice ACKNOWLEDGE, renewal acceptance and COMPLETE with successor validation, closure CLOSE.
13. The Individual Owner upstream-schema tripwire failed in CI once the new producers published schemas. Updated (see section 7).
