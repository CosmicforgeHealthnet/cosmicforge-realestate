# Payment remedies, customer funding and reliability

This extends the existing Financial Core and Sections 4–6 workflows. All local provider operations use explicitly approved **test-mode** accounts. Real provider sandbox, refunds, payouts, DVA eligibility, safeguarding, staging and production remain uncertified. Section 9.2 is **DESIGN RESERVED / EXECUTION DEFERRED**; Section 11 is outside this implementation.

## Deposits, claims and disputes

The approved commercial pack and immutable remedy supplement supply jurisdiction, allowed deposit types, evidence issuers, claim/challenge/review/refund/escalation/notice deadlines, bounded reserve extensions, cooling and dual-approval threshold. There is no guessed legal deadline. Production policy packs require separate legal approval.

Register a canonical allocation liability with its custody/ingress and immutable inspection references. Accepted types are residential security, hospitality damage, event cleaning/damage, land restoration and policy-refundable reserve. Ordinary rent, tax and processing expense cannot be registered as a refundable deposit. Deposit money remains the payer's segregated liability; registration does not certify lawful custody.

A claim or dispute locks the allocation line and freezes only the contested amount, consuming its protected amount before available proceeds. Concurrent claims cannot reserve the same balance. Legacy full-line holds cannot bypass a remedy already recorded on that line. The configured uncontested-release rule leaves unrelated balances eligible for their normal release requirements.

Challenges append payer-authorized facts. Independent Admin review precedes execution. A recorded decision retains claimant, reviewer, executor, approval, evidence and reason references. High-value decisions require distinct actors. Return-to-payer money remains refund-pending; a partial award creates a derived, already-funded protected obligation, with no PSP charge. The beneficiary still needs ordinary release evidence, verification, approval and payout controls. Reserve extension, rework and legal escalation retain contested money. An expired review requires a privileged, dual-approved, evidence-backed, policy-bounded override. An override cannot rewrite a closed case or move money.

## Refund recovery

1. Approve a case decision and reserve each individual refund against the original intent and payer. Confirmed and active reservations together cannot exceed the decision or original payment after prior reversals.
2. Submit a reserved original-instrument refund once. The durable SUBMITTED fact precedes the HTTP request. Acceptance, timeout, generic failure and processing are not proof of completion.
3. Resolve through the original provider, original charge, durable attempt marker and confirmed provider refund ID. Verify amount, currency, merchant and mode. The terminal journal and event commit together.
4. Unknown results retain money. Never submit a replacement refund or switch to wallet credit because a provider call timed out. A generic Paystack failed result is not treated as a confirmed no-effect failure.
5. Only an unsubmitted RESERVED attempt may be explicitly cancelled; it restores the approved refund source, not spendable customer cash. Expiry blocks a new submission and retains money for review. Expiry never cancels an external attempt.

The activated local strategies are original-instrument provider refund and approved customer wallet credit. Provider reversal, verified-bank and manual regulated remedy are policy reservations which fail closed until an appropriate approved execution rail exists. Wallet credit requires an owned CUSTOMER wallet, verified settled funding and no ambiguous provider refund for the original payment. Customer cash-out remains disabled. The adapter's refund discovery uses bounded complete pagination and the original transaction plus attempt marker; it never repeats POST after an ambiguous result.

The implementation follows [Paystack Refund API](https://paystack.com/docs/api/refund/) and [refund lifecycle documentation](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/refunds/). HTTP fixtures are simulations, not sandbox certification.

## Chargebacks and later reversals

An authoritative original-provider economic observation appends a compensating journal. A resolved Paystack dispute alone is insufficient: the adapter requires its associated processed refund, matching the original transaction, merchant, mode, amount and currency. The economic refund reference deduplicates overlapping callbacks. See [Dispute API](https://paystack.com/docs/api/dispute/) and [dispute management](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/manage-disputes/).

Eligible retained protected/available money is consumed first. A spent top-up removes remaining customer availability; a spent amount becomes a recoverable requiring liability review. Paid or in-flight payouts are never invented back into cash. Missing cash becomes explicit PROVIDER_PAYABLE, rather than a negative or fabricated settled-cash balance. A compensating debit can reduce already-restricted availability, but cannot credit spendable availability through that exception.

Recoverable actions append Admin dual approval, current business evidence, risk, reason and liability assignment. Assignment must identify an original beneficiary; top-up liability may be assigned only to its canonical customer. Unassigned debt is not silently charged to an employee, manager or customer. Top-up review evidence uses `topup:<topup UUID>` as its business reference. Platform liability and legal escalation are explicit actions.

Approved recovery debits only a funded internal account with dimension `approved-recovery-reserve:<reference>`, or policy-permitted retained proceeds of the assigned beneficiary; it credits the existing recoverable asset. Source and asset caps prevent over-recovery. Deposits and tax are excluded from proceeds recovery. Outstanding assigned debt blocks ordinary beneficiary release and withdrawal; retained future proceeds require approved recovery review. Recovery does not assert a provider cash receipt or settle PROVIDER_PAYABLE. External repayment needs separate verified provider/bank evidence and reconciliation.

A later confirmed payout reversal preserves the original CONFIRMED_PAID attempt and paid counter, and appends cash-to-FROZEN compensation. A later refund reversal preserves the original refund completion and returns money to payer FROZEN. Read projections show REVERSED. These returned amounts require review; they are not automatically spendable or refunded twice.

## Destinations and payout authority

Wallet role, verified legal party, ownership/mandate, component authority, amount limits and current KYC remain mandatory. Managers receive only their authorized fee components. Corporate/business beneficiaries require a recorded approval chain, two approvers and the exact accepted business/project reference. Configured consent and artisan/professional work-mandate references are additionally required. A mandate for business work is not a recurring-payment execution mandate.

Verification creates an immutable destination version with predecessor, bank ownership, step-up, risk and notification references. Notification is a durable application event/reference; this task sends no external message. Approved policy controls cooling. The previous canonical bank remains usable during ordinary cooling; high-risk pending changes block withdrawals throughout cooling. A second unversioned bank cannot bypass the first canonical bank. Revocation is append-only. The current bank, identity, authority, recipient binding and exact unused approval are rechecked when reserving money.

Payout states are RESERVED → SUBMITTING → PROCESSING/OTP_REQUIRED/UNKNOWN_OUTCOME/REVIEW_REQUIRED → externally CONFIRMED_PAID or confirmed no-effect CONFIRMED_FAILED. Submission responses cannot complete accounting. Later reversal is an appended projection. Unknown, fee-unknown, mismatched and timeout results retain reservation and original provider/reference. Recovery never initiates another transfer.

Availability is a currency/pool-specific projection: AVAILABLE, WITHDRAWABLE_NOW, PROTECTED, SETTLEMENT_PENDING, PAYOUT_PENDING, RESERVED, RAIL_UNAVAILABLE and RESTRICTED are distinct. Withdrawable-now additionally requires operational approved rail, fee capacity, current canonical destination, current authority/KYC, line balance/counters and an unused approval. CUSTOMER cash-out is always false. The older balance `withdrawable` field remains conservative; clients should use the availability endpoint for current eligibility.

## Customer funds and accepted obligations

Only an authenticated owned marketplace CUSTOMER with current verification may request a consented, expiring top-up under an approved CUSTOMER_FUNDING pack. It uses the canonical original checkout intent or an exact authoritative DVA receipt. Unmatched DVA money is retained as an observation; sender-name guesses and administrator-selected wallets are forbidden. Resolving unmatched money requires the normal verified DVA mapping and dual-reviewed original-reference readback.

Collection is pending money. Settled customer funding requires exact original settlement membership, fees, merchant/mode, cash attestation and sufficient pool coverage. The immutable fee policy chooses CUSTOMER_NET or independently covered PLATFORM_FUNDED credit. Economic receipt, top-up and once-only funding facts prevent duplicate credit. Expired funding remains pending for review. Funding and lawful custody remain separate facts.

Wallet spend requires the exact accepted quote version, payer, consent, amount, currency, policy and components. One transaction locks money, debits customer AVAILABLE, creates a canonical internal funded intent, and runs the same allocation validator used by external collections. Every quote has one economic spend; repeated keys replay and altered keys/body conflict. Concurrent different obligations cannot spend the same balance. Failure rolls back the complete obligation and debit. Recipient proceeds become protected liabilities; owner release and platform earning remain separate.

There is no automatic wallet debit, saved-bank/card charge, direct debit, executable mandate, automatic rent collection, customer cash-out or recurring worker. The recurring-design response contains descriptive consent/obligation/notice flags and `execution: DEFERRED` only. Existing manual rent obligation generation is retained.

## Reliability, reconciliation and alerts

The original-reference readback queue covers COLLECTION, PAYOUT and REFUND. Configure `PAYMENT_READBACK_MAX_ATTEMPTS`, `PAYMENT_READBACK_BASE_SECONDS`, `PAYMENT_READBACK_MAX_BACKOFF_SECONDS` and `PAYMENT_READBACK_LEASE_SECONDS`; positive bounded values are required. Local defaults are 12, 5, 3600 and 30. They are engineering defaults, not approved production policy. Lease expiry at exhausted attempt count dead-letters the job. Immutable history retains every claimed attempt and operator replay. Admin replay requires an authority/evidence reference and preserves the original provider and reference.

The durable provider inbox retains normalized facts, signature validation, bounded retries and dead letters. Unsupported or unconfirmed events fail retryably instead of being discarded. Duplicate economic observations cannot duplicate journals. Existing outbox leases, retry/backoff and dead-letter controls publish only committed envelopes. Submission state changes also publish transactionally before provider calls. SQL triggers retain prior/resulting financial states and append audit facts even for intermediate compare-and-swap transitions.

Run reconciliation with approved authority and configurable stale threshold. It compares original collection/payout/refund contexts, terminal journals, settlement membership/fees, coverage, merchant balance, unmatched DVA money, stale outcomes, dead letters, negative controlled balances and unassigned recoverables. Bounded batches explicitly report their limit. Findings remain immutable and do not correct money. A confirmed merchant-cash deficit restricts the pool. Resolving a finding requires a later reconciliation that clears that exact finding plus dual authority/evidence; it appends a resolution. Restrictions are not silently removed by exception resolution.

Admin metrics expose deposits/disputes, claim age, refund reservations/unknowns, chargebacks/recoverables, bank changes/cooling, unknown payouts, pending top-ups, unmatched money, unresolved reconciliation findings, inbox/readback/outbox dead letters and wallet-payment failures. Monetary keys include currency suffixes; an unsuffixed amount is supplied only in a single-currency deployment. `money_currency: MIXED` prohibits adding money across currencies. An alerting integration should page on new dead letters, a cash deficit, a provider-context mismatch, growing unknown reservations or stale claim age; thresholds and routing must come from approved operational policy. Metrics and existing observability hooks are local primitives; deployment monitoring remains uncertified.

For an incident, pause new financial commands/affected rails, preserve receipts, journals, case facts, raw signed callback evidence and original references, and read back before acting. A reconciliation discrepancy or lack of callback is never permission to resubmit money. Replay only the retained original job after evidence review. Use a compensating approved workflow for corrections; never edit or delete historical financial records.

## Recovery and secrets

Deployment owners must approve RPO/RTO, encrypted PostgreSQL base backups plus WAL/PITR, off-host retention, restore drills, key recovery, and JetStream durability. Restore to an isolated environment with financial workers and outbound provider submission disabled. Check migration version, journal balance, controlled balances, immutable receipts/attempts, coverage, inbox/outbox leases and provider original references before resuming. A database restore can revive an already-completed external attempt: use readback, never automatic re-initiation. Reconcile actual provider/bank outcomes and obtain dual approval before enabling rails. Local migration round-trip, retention rejection, crash-lease and replay tests are not a production PITR drill.

Only secret references, public signing-key metadata and normalized provider facts are durable. Runtime provider keys use secret wrappers. Up to two prior test-mode webhook keys may overlap during rotation; remove the overlap after the approved window. Service-key verification and provider-webhook rotation are separate controls. Credential references must be environment-variable names; key material, card numbers, CVV, PIN, bank-login secrets and primary inspection documents are not accepted in these DTOs. Strict request decoding, audience-bound service authentication, gateway delegation, owned wallets, Admin/Verification roles and bounded bodies remain enforced. Logging uses identifiers and safe error codes; backups and callback evidence need appropriate access and retention controls.
